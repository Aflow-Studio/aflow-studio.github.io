Four games

One obsession: rarer jewels

Each game has its own twist, and every one of them pays out in rubies and black diamonds.

The Pouch

Drop jewels in a leather pouch. Two alike merge into a rarer gem. Don't let it overflow.

The Vault

Turn the rings of a safe door and line up twins. 25 moves to crack it.

The Haul

Swipe through matching jewels. The longer the chain, the rarer the gem.

The Loot

Place stacks on the display. Four alike on top merge. Keep cells free.
